# news-image-20261124 — Round 2 Entry

**ONE DAY, ONE SKY** — A dense cream broadsheet capturing every major story of Tuesday 24 November 2026 in a single 1024×1024 image.

## The Twist
Three lead illustrations share ONE continuous sky gradient: dawn gold over Santa Clara (Nvidia HQ) → midday blue over London (Gaza children evacuation) → night black over orbit (Russian nuclear orbiter). The masthead subhead carries it: *"ONE DAY, ONE SKY — DAWN IN CALIFORNIA · NOON IN LONDON · NIGHT IN ORBIT."*

- **3 lead panels** with detailed drawn scenes (Nvidia glass tower + palms + sun, London hospital + ambulance + Big Ben silhouette, orbit satellite + Earth limb + starfield)
- **THE DAY IN TEN** — 10 numbered cards with mini-illustrations
- **WHERE IT HAPPENED** — dotted world map with 13 numbered red pins matching story locations
- **ALSO ON** — 22 more stories in two columns (items 14–35)
- **DATE CHECK** box naming 10 deliberately left-out stories from prior days
- **Tag legend** (ONGOING / REPORTED / CLAIM / OFFICIALS SAY)
- **Footer** with 7 source credits
